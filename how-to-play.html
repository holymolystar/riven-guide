How to play Riven

Short, explosive trades built on animation cancels, then snowball the lead.

01Trading pattern

  • Weave autos

    Auto between every ability; skipping them throws away your passive damage.

  • Short trades

    Go in, combo, and back off while your Q is on cooldown.

  • Shield the return damage

    Save E to soak what they hit back with.

  • No mana

    You can trade every time your cooldowns are up.

  • Q delay

    Once you have enough ability haste, hold your Q charges between casts to force all-ins in almost every matchup.

  • Bush cheese

    In most lanes your level 1 all-in from the lane bush is your best chance to snowball.

  • End trades with Conqueror stacked,

    Then back off while your cooldowns come back. Use Ignite just before your stacks run out to keep them up.

  • Ping-pong

    Weave in and out of fights instead of standing in them.

02Power spikes

  • Level 3

    All three basic abilities unlock your real trades.

  • Level 6

    Your R is your biggest spike. Force fights in the window when your R is back up.

  • Axiom Arc

    More R uptime for skirmishes and split pushing.

  • Q timing

    The charges reset if you wait too long, so don't hold them.

  • First back

    Boots and a Glowing Mote for an early spike; early boots also fill Jack Of All Trades.

03Lane and map

  • Play for the early lead

    Riven snowballs harder than most top laners.

  • Hop walls with Q3

    To escape or to cut through the jungle.

  • Don't overcommit

    When your E and Q are both down.

04Teamfight role

  • Wait for their CC

    Let key stuns and displacements go out before you commit.

  • Shield first

    Use E to take the first piece of CC or burst.

  • Flank

    Come in from an angle, not the front.

  • Save Wind Slash

    For low-health targets; it hits harder the lower they are.
